# The family pulse

The line under the circle name at the top of the map tells you how the people in the circle are doing, without opening anyone.

| It says | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone is up to date | Every other person's phone is reporting normally |
| Maya quiet 40 min | Maya's phone has not reported for 40 minutes (shown in amber) |
| Sam driving | Sam is in a car right now |
| 3 quiet | Three phones have gone quiet |

When you are alone in a circle, or have paused sharing, the line keeps saying "Location sharing: on" or "paused".

## Asking a quiet phone to update

Tap the quiet person on the map or in the list. Their panel shows "location isn't updating" with an **Ask to update** button. It asks that phone for a fresh position; if the phone is reachable the map updates within a minute or so. If it stays quiet, the phone is probably off, out of signal, or Android stopped the app on it (see [Battery and permissions](battery-and-permissions.md)).
