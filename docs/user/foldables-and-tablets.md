# Orbitly on foldables and tablets

How Orbitly looks and behaves on a folding phone such as the Galaxy Z Fold, on tablets, and in split-screen.

Orbitly changes its layout to fit the space it has. You do not have to set anything.

| Space | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Narrow (a normal phone, the Fold's cover screen, a half-width window) | The map fills the screen. People and places are in a sheet you pull up from the bottom. The four main tabs are in a bar at the bottom. |
| Wide (the Fold's inner screen, a tablet, a phone turned sideways) | The map sits on the left and the People and Places list stays open on the right. The four main tabs move to a bar on the left edge. |

## Folding and unfolding

You can fold or unfold the phone while Orbitly is open. The map stays where it was (same position and zoom), your
place in each tab is kept, and nothing reloads or reconnects. The layout simply rearranges itself.

The same applies when you turn the phone sideways or drag the divider in split-screen or a pop-up window.

## Settings on a narrow screen

Settings, Notifications and Link another phone are laid out for the Fold's cover display (about 344 dp wide) and
for large text: the icons in front of the rows are left out so the words and the switch get the room, and long text
wraps. On wide screens they sit in a centred column no wider than about 600 dp. Other screens have not been
checked at 344 dp yet.

## Tips

- Orbitly works in any direction. There is no portrait lock.
- If you use a larger text size in your phone's settings, the layouts adjust. If something looks cut off at your
  size, tell us which phone and which text size.
- The map follows the 120 Hz screen on phones that have one. Moving members glide instead of jumping.
