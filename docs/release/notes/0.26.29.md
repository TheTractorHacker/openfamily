# Orbitly 0.26.29

**App update only.** The server needs no change. On iPhone, let the Apple Watch app update along with it.

## New
- **Settings is in six groups:** Account, Circle, Notifications, Privacy and security, Devices, About. The things most people never need (Diagnostics, Server address, Map style, Delete account) are folded under **Advanced**. The search box at the top finds every setting, Advanced ones too.
- **Fits narrow screens.** Settings, Notifications and Link another phone now fit the Galaxy Z Fold's cover display and large text: the little icons give way to the words.
- **Link another phone, redone.** It asks "Which phone is this?" and then shows one step at a time with one button.
- **Keep me signed in says what it does** and whether it is working right now. It cannot work with two-factor sign-in, and the row says so.
- **Apple Watch: the direction arrow turns with your wrist**, like Precision Finding. While the direction screen is open the position refreshes every 2 seconds, and the rest of the watch app is less laggy.
- **Error messages say what is actually wrong.** "This phone is offline" only appears when the phone really has no network.

## Fixed
- **Quiet hours no longer pops in and out** when you scroll the notification settings up and down.
- Settings draws much less while you scroll.

## Not tried on a real device yet
Everything above was tested on a computer only. We have not seen any of it on a real phone, a folded Fold or an Apple Watch, so smoothness and battery use on the watch are unproven. Other screens (member detail, places, driving reports) can still overflow on the 344 dp cover display and are next. If anything looks cramped or lags, send Diagnostics from **Settings > Advanced**.
