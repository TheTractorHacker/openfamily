Orbitly is a private way for family and friends to see where each other are, get arrival alerts and send an SOS. Your data lives on a server your own circle controls. There is no Orbitly cloud, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

IMPORTANT: Orbitly needs an Orbitly server. Someone in your circle (or you) runs one and gives you its address and an invite. To try the app first, the review notes name a demo server.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Live map: see where each person in your circle is, how they are moving and their battery level.
- Places and alerts: get a notification when someone arrives at or leaves home, school or work.
- SOS and "I'm OK": one tap tells your whole circle where you are. Orbitly does not call emergency services. In an emergency call your local emergency number first.
- Check-ins: let your circle know you arrived safely.
- Circle chat with photos.
- Weekly trips and driving report, computed from location.
- Apple Watch app: see your people, send "I'm OK" or SOS and send quick replies from your wrist (needs the iPhone).

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
- Your information is stored on the server of your own circle, not with the app developer.
- Private circles are end-to-end encrypted: locations, places, alerts and chat are locked on your iPhone and opened only on your circle's phones.
- You decide what to share, can pause sharing at any time, and can download or delete your data inside the app.
- Connections use HTTPS only.

LOCATION IN THE BACKGROUND
To keep your circle's map current while the app is closed, Orbitly uses your location in the background, only after you turn on sharing and choose "Always". iOS shows a blue indicator while it does. You can change this at any time in Settings.

Open source software for families who want to keep each other safe without handing their lives to a company.
