# Orbitly 0.26.31

**App update only.** The server needs no change. Update the Wear OS watch app along with it.

## New
- **Wear OS: the direction arrow turns smoothly with your wrist**, the way it does on the Apple Watch. It follows your movement with no steps, always turns the short way round, and a small mark on the ring shows north.
- **The arrow points to true north**, not magnetic north, so it is no longer a few degrees off in places where the two differ.
- **A steadier arrow when you raise your wrist**, and a hint to **move your wrist in a figure 8** (the arrow fades) when the watch compass needs calibrating.
- **Fresher position while you look for someone:** with the direction screen open the watch asks your phone for the other person's position every 2 seconds, and a new position glides in instead of making the arrow jump.

## Not tried on a real device yet
Everything above was tested on a computer only. We have not seen it on a real Wear OS watch, so how smooth it feels, how it behaves on different watches and wrists, and the battery use while the direction screen is open are unproven. The compass only runs while that screen is open and awake. If the arrow behaves oddly, send Diagnostics from **Settings > Advanced**.
