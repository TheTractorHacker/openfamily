# Orbitly 0.26.30

**App update only.** The server needs no change. Update the Wear OS watch app along with the phone app.

## New
- **Cleaner screens that line up.** Trips, Security, Encryption keys, Link another phone, the circle processing rules and the circle list on the map were redrawn: one left edge, even rows, one short line under each title, and the long explanations behind the (i) button.
- **Trips:** Distance, Duration, Top speed and Average now sit in equal columns (two and two on a narrow screen). The Driving report row and the "Processed by server" label scroll with the list.
- **Wear OS: the direction arrow turns smoothly with your wrist**, the way it does on the Apple Watch. It points to true north, steadies when you raise your wrist, and asks you to move your wrist in a figure 8 when the compass needs calibrating. While the direction screen is open the position refreshes every 2 seconds.

## Fixed
- **Samsung "Never sleeping apps" didn't list Orbitly.** The battery button now opens a one-tap system question for Orbitly itself, or Orbitly's own app page. On Samsung you can also go to **Settings > Apps > Orbitly > Battery > Unrestricted**, which does the same job.
- Wording that promised more than the app does ("Positions are not delivered until then") now says "may not arrive".

## Not tried on a real device yet
Everything above was tested on a computer only. We have not seen it on a real phone, a folded Fold or a Wear OS watch, so how the screens look with your fonts, how smooth the arrow feels and the battery use on the watch are unproven. Other screens (member detail, places, driving reports) can still overflow on the 344 dp cover display and are next. If anything looks off, send Diagnostics from **Settings > Advanced**.
