# Orbitly 0.26.33

**App update only.** The server needs no change.

## What this is about
On one phone, sharing stopped the moment the app left the screen while driving. The most likely reason is that Android ended Orbitly's background service. **If Android ends the app, this update cannot prevent it.** What it does is recover sooner and tell us why next time.

## New
- **The background service is started again when you open the app.** If sharing is on, "Allow all the time" is granted, and the service is not running, Orbitly starts it. It never starts when sharing is off or the permission is missing.
- **Diagnostics now says why Android ended the app.** The report has a **Recent process exits** list (for example frozen, out of memory, closed by you, crash) with the time, plus the battery bucket Android put Orbitly in.
- **Diagnostics keeps a short journal** of the tracking service (started, stopped, restarted, app paused and resumed) and per-hour counts of positions received, sent and kept back, with the last error type. It has no locations in it.

## Fixed
- **Positions kept back because the phone could not read its key are sent later by themselves.** The background part now tries again at every ten-minute check and sends them once the key can be read, instead of keeping them until you open the app.

## If tracking stopped
Check Location is **Allow all the time**, battery is **Unrestricted**, and on Samsung that Orbitly is not in Sleeping apps. Then send the report from **Settings > Advanced > Diagnostics > Copy diagnostic report**.

## Not tried on a real device yet
Nothing in this update was run on a phone. It was checked on a computer only, and the Android parts were not built into an app. We do not know yet whether this phone's problem is the one described.
